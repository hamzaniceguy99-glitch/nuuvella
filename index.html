How we work

A coach, not a research service

We don't do the research for you. Partly because it's the enjoyable part, and partly because the skills transfer — every brick wall you break teaches you how to break the next one.

We're also careful with two things: we never interpret health information from DNA tests, and we treat living relatives' privacy and any unexpected findings as the serious matters they are.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Do I need a paid subscription?

It helps a great deal for census and vital records, but a lot is free — FamilySearch, state archives, and many libraries offer access. We'll show you what's free first.

My family came from a country whose records I can't read.

Common and workable. We cover finding translated indexes, working with archives abroad, and reading enough of a language to extract names, dates and places.

Can you tell me about my DNA health results?

No, never. We only work with DNA at a genealogical level. Health interpretation belongs with a doctor or a genetic counselor.

I found something upsetting in my results.

It happens more than people expect — unexpected parentage in particular. We'll help you think through the research side carefully, and we'll suggest proper support rather than pretending coaching is therapy.

Will you find my ancestors?

We'll teach you to search well, but no honest genealogist guarantees a find. Some records burned, some were never created.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.
